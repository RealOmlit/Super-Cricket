A GAME OF TIMING

Own the crease.

Choose your teams, target, overs and CPU difficulty in Match setup. Six legal balls make an over; batters change strike after odd runs and each over. Running is automatic. Pick your shot, find a gap, and meet the ball at the crease.

  1. 01

    Choose your direction

    Use A D or the arrow buttons to aim into the field.

  2. 02

    Keep it down. Or send it.

    Ground shots travel along the grass. Switch to Lofted with L to go over the field.

  3. 03

    Timing is everything

    Press SPACE or Play shot as the marker reaches the sweet spot. Early or late swings can cost a wicket.
